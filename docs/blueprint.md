# **App Name**: MowerFlow

## Core Features:

- Lead Capture: Capture leads from web forms, Facebook ads, and missed calls, automatically creating tickets in the system.
- AI-Powered Qualification: An AI bot interacts with leads via voice or SMS, asking custom questions to qualify leads and tag them based on urgency and service type, serving as a tool for front-office automation.
- Smart Scheduling: Integrates with Google Calendar and Cal.com to display real-time availability and allows one-tap scheduling.
- Digital Job Sheets: Provides checklist templates per service (e.g., mower blade inspection, cart battery water level) for consistent and thorough job execution.
- Invoicing and Payment: Generates PDF invoices on-site and facilitates card payments using the phone camera via Stripe Terminal SDK.
- Automated Follow-up: Automatically sends Google review requests 24 hours after a job is marked complete.
- Equipment Registry: Allows storage of equipment details like mower model, serial number, and engine hours using photo and OCR technology.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Forest Green (#388E3C) to reflect the lawn care industry and a sense of growth and reliability.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC), offering a soft, neutral backdrop that complements the primary color without being visually distracting.
- Accent color: Mustard Yellow (#FFB300) to highlight important actions and information, providing contrast and capturing attention.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' for a modern and clean look that ensures readability and professional appeal.
- Use clear and representative icons for different services and functions, enhancing user navigation and visual understanding.
- A clean, intuitive layout optimized for mobile devices, ensuring easy access to key features for technicians in the field.
- Subtle transitions and animations to provide feedback and enhance user experience, ensuring a smooth and engaging interface.
